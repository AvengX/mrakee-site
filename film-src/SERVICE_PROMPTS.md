# Service images — 3 prompts for Google Flow

The Services section now uses the same alternating rows as Solutions, so
it needs three images of its own. Until they exist each row falls back to
a still from the film, so the section works today — these just make it
match the quality of the Solutions rows.

## How to hand them back

Save as **`01.jpg`, `02.jpg`, `03.jpg`** and drop into:

```
D:\STUDY\PP\kiosk-scroll\public\services\
```

Picked up automatically, same as the solution images. Don't resize or
crop — I'll do the watermark crop and the compression.

## Same rules as before

- **16:9 landscape.**
- **Keep the bottom-right corner clear** — the Flow watermark sits around
  x 95–100%, y 86–96% on stills and I crop it off.
- **No real brand names or logos** anywhere.
- **Minimal on-screen text** — generators produce convincing gibberish.

These three are about *people doing work*, not about screens. That is the
point of the section — the screen is the easy part — so unlike the
solution images, the display should usually be secondary or absent.

## Shared style block — append to every prompt

```
Photorealistic corporate documentary photography, bright and airy, natural
daylight, soft neutral palette of white, pale grey and warm timber. Shot on
a full-frame camera at 35mm, f/2.8, shallow depth of field. Candid and
unposed, real working moment, not a stock handshake. Clean modern
architecture, uncluttered, premium but understated. No visible brand names,
logos or trademarks. Minimal on-screen text. Nothing important in the
bottom-right corner. 16:9 landscape.
```

## The 3 prompts

**01 — Technical Consulting**
> Two colleagues surveying a large empty retail space, one holding a tablet
> showing a simple floor plan and pointing up at a wall where a display will
> be mounted, the other looking where they are pointing. Bare architecture,
> daylight through a shopfront, work in progress.

**02 — Creative Services**
> A designer at a wide desk reviewing digital signage layouts, a large
> monitor showing simple bold poster compositions in a grid, printed layout
> proofs spread on the desk beside it, a bright modern studio with plants
> and daylight behind.

**03 — Support & Maintenance**
> A technician in a plain work shirt kneeling beside an open service panel at
> the base of a digital display in a quiet mall after hours, a small laptop
> balanced on one knee, calm concentration, low warm ambient light with the
> panel casting a soft glow.

## If they come out wrong

1. **Too staged** — add "candid, caught mid-task, nobody looking at camera".
2. **Too dark** (especially 03) — add "high-key, bright exposure, clearly lit
   despite the evening setting". It still has to sit on a white page.
3. **Cluttered** — add "minimal, uncluttered, generous empty space".
4. **Faces distorted** — pull the camera back: "full-body, mid-distance,
   faces small in frame".
