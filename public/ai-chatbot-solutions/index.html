Frequently Asked Questions About AI Chatbot Solutions

Common questions about MRakee Technologies' conversational assistants and interactive docks.

Where can MRakee Technologies conversational assistants be deployed?

Our assistants can be deployed across interactive smart kiosks, terminal touchscreens, corporate websites, and facility reception desks to assist visitors and automate customer inquiries.

Can MRakee Technologies AI assistants support voice and text?

Yes. Our solutions support multimodal interaction including voice input via microphone arrays, natural speech synthesis, on-screen text transcripts, and direct touch selections.

How can an AI assistant be integrated into a kiosk or website?

We integrate assistants via docked floating widgets, dedicated full-screen kiosk applications, or embedded web frames connected to your organization's verified service documentation and contact routing.

How does the assistant handle complex inquiries?

When an inquiry exceeds automated boundaries, the assistant captures the visitor's contact details and requirements, then seamlessly routes the lead directly to your sales or support team.
