Digital Signage Capabilities

We manage the complete digital signage journey: concept evaluation, display hardware specification, secure distribution architecture, professional installation, and ongoing maintenance.

01 / CENTRALIZED CONTROL

Network Content Management

Centrally managed software ecosystems that allow administrators to schedule, update, and distribute multimedia messaging across individual screens, campus zones, or regional display networks.

02 / LARGE DISPLAYS

Video Walls & Large Format Displays

High-impact LED video walls and large-format visual displays for corporate lobbies, auditoriums, experience centres, and control rooms where maximum visual clarity is essential.

03 / CONTINUOUS OPERATION

Commercial-Grade Hardware

Reliable commercial displays, media players, and mounting hardware engineered specifically for high-duty-cycle environments, continuous 24/7 runtimes, and wide viewing angles.

04 / INTERACTIVE SIGNAGE

Wayfinding & Touch Displays

Interactive touch displays and digital directory podiums designed to help visitors navigate campuses, healthcare facilities, retail complexes, and transportation terminals with ease.

05 / DISTRIBUTION

Coordinated Signal Distribution

Engineered AV-over-IP and centralized signal distribution that ensures synchronized visual output, low-latency transmission, and clean cabling infrastructure across architectural spaces.

MRAKEE Care Maintenance

Preventive maintenance, display recalibration, firmware management, and rapid support to minimize downtime and protect your visual technology investments over the long term.
